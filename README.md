## Games (for Kids)

Interactive educational games:
- **Princess Memory Match** - Royal-themed card matching
- **Emoji & Word Match** - Match emojis to words
- **Word Adventure** - Explore worlds through word matching
- **Reading Quest** - Phonics and letter learning

## Travel Tools

Trip planning utilities:
- **Pinterest Packing List** - Photo upload + AI styling suggestions
- **Simple Packing List** - Quick packing checklist
- **Travel Schedule** - Family trip scheduler

## Other Tools

Personal dashboards and utilities:
- **Prose & Rosé** - Reading dashboard: 447 books across 6 years, genre and theme analytics,
  TBR pile, Recently Read with wine + song pairings. Older versions live in `archive/`.
- **Gift Genius** - Gift idea tracker (password-protected)
- **Volunteer Tracker**, **Fridge Chef**, **Spanish / Italian Trainer**
